# EIMAGE privacy policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026.

EIMAGE is a free, MIT-licensed desktop screenshot editor. This policy describes the implemented application. Microsoft Store account, payment, diagnostics and update services are governed separately by Microsoft's policies.

## Images and screen access

EIMAGE captures a screen or window only when you choose a capture action. You can also open a local PNG, JPEG or WebP. These images and the editor's undo history are processed in local application memory. EIMAGE does not upload screenshots, original images, filenames or edits to the developer or an application server.

Saving creates a local image at the location you choose. Copying places an image in the system clipboard. Other applications may read the clipboard; its history and synchronization follow your operating system settings. Saved files, original files and clipboard copies have lifetimes separate from the editor. Clearing the editor releases its current document and history; it does not erase those independent copies or OS caches.

## Storage and telemetry

The app stores a light/dark theme preference in the local webview profile. It does not persist image history, create an account, include analytics or advertising, load remote fonts, or run application telemetry. The operating system and Microsoft's WebView2 runtime may provide their own updates or diagnostics outside EIMAGE's control.

## Concealment

Opaque covers are flattened into exported pixels. Originals, undo history and earlier copies can still contain covered information. Inspect the entire result before sharing. EIMAGE does not promise forensic anonymization.

## External links and verification

Following a source, support or verification link opens an external service with its own policy. EIMAGE does not automatically submit images or installers to VirusTotal. Do not include private screenshots in public support issues.

## Contact and changes

Use [the project's support issues](https://github.com/AlgoWolfx/EImage/issues) for non-sensitive privacy questions. For a sensitive concern, request a private contact method without posting personal data. Changes to this policy are recorded in the repository. There is no developer-hosted account or image database to request deletion from; you control your saved images, originals and clipboard on your device.
